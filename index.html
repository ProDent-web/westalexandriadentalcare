Frequently asked

Good questions deserve clear answers.

Still unsure? Call us at 703-263-8719 or browse all questions and answers.

Are you accepting new patients?

Yes. West Alexandria Dental Care is welcoming new patients for our October 2026 opening. Book online at any hour, or call 703-263-8719 and the team will find a time that fits, including evenings and weekends.

Do you see both adults and children?

Yes. This is a comprehensive family office for every stage of life, from a child's first visit to implants and dentures for grandparents, with records in one place so care stays joined up.

Which insurance plans do you accept?

Aetna, Ameritas, Delta Dental, Humana, Cigna, MetLife and Virginia Medicaid. Participation and benefits vary by employer and plan, so send us your card through the registration page or bring it along and we will check your benefits before anything starts.

What if I feel anxious about dental care?

Tell us when you book. We plan a slower, step-by-step visit, agree a signal to pause, and offer nitrous oxide and other sedation options after an individual health review. A first visit can be just a conversation.

What languages does your team speak?

English, Arabic, Spanish and Portuguese. Mention your preferred language when you request an appointment so we can schedule the right team member for you.

Do you place dental implants?

Yes. We plan complete tooth replacement here, including bridges, dentures and implant-supported options when they are the right fit. Dr. Fadi Alhrashi completed a dental implant fellowship at Tufts and is a Diplomate of the American Board of Oral Implantology.

Where is the office and is there parking?

We are in the Bradlee Office Building at 3543 W. Braddock Road, Suite E1, Alexandria, VA 22302, steps from Bradlee Shopping Center and just off I-395 at the King Street exit. Free surface parking surrounds the building.
