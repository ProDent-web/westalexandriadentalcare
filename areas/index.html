Areas we serve

A dental home for West Alexandria and the neighborhoods around it

The office sits in the Bradlee Office Building on W. Braddock Road, steps from Bradlee Shopping Center and just off I-395 at the King Street exit, with free surface parking around the building. That puts it a few minutes from Seminary Hill, Fairlington, Shirlington and Parkfairfax, and an easy drive from Del Ray, Landmark, Lincolnia, Bailey's Crossroads and South Arlington.

Why the location works

Most of the people who come here are fitting dentistry around something else: a commute on I-395 or the Metro, school drop-off, shift work, a condo in Fairlington or Parkfairfax that is closer to this office than to anything in Old Town. Being just off the King Street exit with parking at the door means a 7 AM appointment before work or a 7 PM one after it is realistic rather than theoretical.

Seven-day hours are the other half of it. Saturday and Sunday mornings exist for the families and commuters who cannot make a weekday work. Read more about evening and weekend appointments.

What people from these neighborhoods tend to need

  • One office for the whole household, from a toddler's first visit to implants for grandparents. See family dentistry.
  • Insurance that actually works, including Virginia Medicaid alongside Aetna, Ameritas, Delta Dental, Humana, Cigna and MetLife. See insurance we accept.
  • Care in English, Arabic, Spanish or Portuguese. See languages we speak.
  • Somewhere to go when a tooth breaks on a weekend. See emergency dental care.
  • A fresh start after moving to Alexandria or after years away from the dentist. See switching dentists.

Getting here

  1. From I-395

    Take the King Street exit, head toward Alexandria, and turn onto W. Braddock Road. The Bradlee Office Building is beside Bradlee Shopping Center.

  2. From Quaker Lane or Seminary Road

    Both cross W. Braddock Road within a few minutes of the office, which is the usual route from Fairlington, Parkfairfax and Seminary Hill.

  3. Parking

    Free surface parking around the building. No garage, no validation, no meter.

Not on the list?

The neighborhood pages are starting points, not boundaries. Patients come from across Alexandria, Arlington and Fairfax County, and the welcome is the same wherever you live.
