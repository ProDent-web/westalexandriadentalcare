Your dentist near Shirlington

Shirlington's apartments and condos put a lot of people within a ten-minute drive of our office who do not have a dentist nearby, or who have one that closes at five. From the Village at Shirlington it is one exit down I-395 or a straight run along Quaker Lane to the Bradlee Office Building, where parking is free and the hours run to 8 PM on weekdays and through weekend mornings.

Who we see from Shirlington

Renters and condo owners in their twenties and thirties who moved for the restaurants and the commute, often without a local dentist yet. Dog walkers from the trail. People who work in the District and want an appointment that does not mean leaving the office early. And a fair number who have simply not been to a dentist since they moved to the area and want somewhere to start without a lecture. See switching dentists.

The pattern we see from Shirlington is the evening booking: a 7 PM cleaning on the way home, or a Saturday morning before the farmers' market.

Getting to the office

  1. Via I-395

    South one exit to King Street, toward Alexandria, then W. Braddock Road. The Bradlee Office Building is beside Bradlee Shopping Center.

  2. Via Quaker Lane

    South on Quaker Lane past Fairlington to W. Braddock Road. About ten minutes, depending on traffic.

  3. Via Shirlington Road and King Street

    An alternative when I-395 is slow.

  4. Parking

    Free surface parking around the building. No garage, no validation.

Practical things worth knowing

  • Open 7 AM to 8 PM Monday to Friday and 8 AM to 1 PM on Saturday and Sunday.
  • Aetna, Ameritas, Delta Dental, Humana, Cigna, MetLife and Virginia Medicaid accepted.
  • Care in English, Arabic, Spanish and Portuguese.
  • Paperwork completed online in a few minutes, through the patient registration page.
  • Booking online around the clock, or by phone at 703-263-8719.

New job, new insurance?

Send a photo of your new card through the online registration and we will verify what it covers before you book. If your plan is not on our list, contact us and we will check.

Straight answers

Is it faster to take I-395 or Quaker Lane?

Either is about ten minutes outside rush hour. Quaker Lane is often the steadier choice when I-395 is backed up.

Do I need to be an Alexandria resident?

No. Patients come from Shirlington, South Arlington and across Northern Virginia. Where you live makes no difference to the welcome.

Can I book online for an evening slot?

Yes. Online booking shows real availability including weekday evenings until 8 PM and weekend mornings.

I have not seen a dentist since college. Where do I start?

With a cleaning and exam. Say when you book that it has been a while, and we allow extra time, go gently, and start from where things are. No lecture.
