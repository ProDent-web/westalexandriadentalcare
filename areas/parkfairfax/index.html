Your dentist near Parkfairfax

Parkfairfax's garden condos along Quaker Lane and Martha Custis Drive are about as close to our office as a neighborhood can be without being Seminary Hill. A few minutes south on Quaker Lane brings you to W. Braddock Road and the Bradlee Office Building, with free parking at the door and hours that run from 7 AM to 8 PM on weekdays and through weekend mornings.

Who we see from Parkfairfax

A mix that reflects the neighborhood: first-time buyers who chose Parkfairfax for the price and the trees, long-time residents who have been there for decades, and commuters who use the bus along Quaker Lane or drive to the Pentagon and the District. The long-time residents often arrive with a dentist who retired or moved; the newer ones often arrive with no dentist at all. Both get the same first visit: a conversation, an exam, and a written plan.

Because the drive is so short, Parkfairfax patients tend to use the early slots: a 7 AM cleaning and still at a desk by 8:30.

Getting to the office

  1. Via Quaker Lane

    South on Quaker Lane to W. Braddock Road, then a short distance to the Bradlee Office Building beside Bradlee Shopping Center. A few minutes door to door.

  2. Via Valley Drive and King Street

    An alternative route through the neighborhood to King Street and then W. Braddock Road.

  3. By bus

    Routes along Quaker Lane and King Street stop near Bradlee Shopping Center; check the current schedule.

  4. Parking

    Free surface parking around the building.

Practical things worth knowing

  • Open 7 AM to 8 PM Monday to Friday and 8 AM to 1 PM on Saturday and Sunday.
  • Aetna, Ameritas, Delta Dental, Humana, Cigna, MetLife and Virginia Medicaid accepted.
  • Care in English, Arabic, Spanish and Portuguese.
  • Paperwork completed online before you arrive, through the patient registration page.
  • Booking online around the clock, or by phone at 703-263-8719.

Nervous after a long gap?

Say so when you book. A first visit can be a conversation only, and the exam waits for the second. We plan comfort first.

Nervous about the dentist?

Straight answers

How close is the office to Parkfairfax?

A few minutes south on Quaker Lane. The office is in the Bradlee Office Building on W. Braddock Road, beside Bradlee Shopping Center, with free parking.

Can I get in before work?

Yes. The first appointments start at 7 AM on weekdays, which is one of the most popular slots for Parkfairfax and Fairlington patients.

My dentist retired. Can you get my records?

Yes. You are entitled to your records and X-rays, and we can request them for you from the previous office or whoever took over the practice.

Do you see seniors who have trouble getting around?

Yes. Parking is at the door and the office is in an office building; call ahead and we will help you plan the visit, including a companion in the room.
