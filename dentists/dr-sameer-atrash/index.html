Dr. Sameer Atrash, DMD

Dr. Sameer Atrash, Dr. Sam to his patients, owns West Alexandria Dental Care and is one of its two general dentists. He grew up in Northern Virginia and practices by a simple rule: prevent what can be prevented, preserve what can be preserved.

About Dr. Sameer Atrash

Dr. Sam was born in Washington, D.C. and grew up in Fairfax, Virginia. The interest in dentistry started in high school, through Fairfax County's Dental Careers Program, and it stuck.

At Virginia Commonwealth University he earned a Bachelor of Science in Biology and then a Master of Product Innovation. That second degree is not the usual route into dentistry, and it shows in how he treats a treatment plan: something to think through, explain clearly and get right.

He earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine at Tufts in Boston in 2021. West Alexandria Dental Care is his own practice, opening in October 2026 in the Bradlee Office Building off I-395.

How Dr. Sam works

Prevent what can be prevented, preserve what can be preserved. For Dr. Sam that is the order of operations. The first question about any tooth is whether it needs treatment at all. The second is the smallest repair that will last.

He explains the diagnosis before the treatment. Any digital X-ray or 3D image goes on the screen beside you, in plain words, until it makes sense to you.

Then you decide together. He lays out the options, including leaving things alone for now, with what each involves and what it costs, in writing, before anything starts. If something can safely wait, he says so. If it cannot, he says why.

If you are nervous, say so when you book. He paces the visit, explains each step before it happens, and agrees on a stop signal. Nitrous oxide is available, and other sedation options can be discussed after a health review. More on how this practice works and options for anxious patients.

Clinical interests

Dental implants

From a single missing tooth to an implant-retained denture, planned with 3D imaging so the position is settled before surgery and finished with the crown, bridge or denture on top.

Restorative dentistry

Fillings, onlays, crowns and bridges, chosen by how much healthy tooth each one saves. A crown only when the tooth needs holding together.

Clear aligners, including Invisalign

Removable trays for adults and teens, from the planning visit through to the retainer that keeps the result.

Seeing Dr. Sam

Dr. Sam is accepting new patients. You can book online any time or call 703-263-8719, and ask for him when you schedule.

Hours run 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM Monday through Friday and 8:00 AM to 1:00 PM Saturday and Sunday, so before work or on a weekend morning is a normal appointment here.

Dr. Sam and Dr. Fadi Alhrashi share one office and one set of records, so if you need care on a day Dr. Sam is not in, Dr. Fadi can pick up where he left off. Dr. Fadi sees patients in English and Arabic, and the office covers Spanish and Portuguese too. Mention your preferred language when you book.

Before your first visit, complete the patient registration page online and upload a photo of your insurance card. The office is in the Bradlee Office Building in Alexandria, just off I-395 at the King Street exit, with free parking.

Straight answers

Does Dr. Sam see children?

Yes. This is a family practice, and children come for exams, cleanings, sealants, fluoride and sports mouthguards alongside their parents. If a child ever needs care that belongs with a specialist, we say so and help arrange it. See children's dentistry.

Which dentist should I book with?

Either. Both see new patients and handle the everyday work of check-ups, fillings and crowns. If you are thinking about clear aligners or cosmetic changes, Dr. Sam is a natural fit. For surgical or complex cases, or the dentist with nearly two decades of training and clinical experience, ask for Dr. Fadi. You can switch later.

Does Dr. Fadi place implants here?

Yes. Dr. Fadi completed a Dental Implant Fellowship at Tufts University School of Dental Medicine and is a Diplomate of the American Board of Oral Implantology/Implant Dentistry and a Fellow of the American Academy of Implant Dentistry. Both are general dentists, not specialists. Implant cases are planned here with 3D imaging, and if one belongs with a specialist, you will hear that first.

Does Dr. Sam do Invisalign?

Yes. Clear aligner treatment, including Invisalign, is one of his main clinical interests, for adults and teens. Not every bite suits aligners, and he will say if yours is one for an orthodontist instead. Start with whether clear aligners are right for you.
