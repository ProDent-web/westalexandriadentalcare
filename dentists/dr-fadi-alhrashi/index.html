Meet your dentist

Dr. Fadi Alhrashi, DDS

Dr. Fadi Alhrashi is a general dentist at West Alexandria Dental Care with nearly two decades of advanced training and clinical experience, much of it at the surgical and restorative end of dentistry: implants, extractions, and the complex cases that need a careful plan. He is calm, and he explains before he treats.

About Dr. Fadi Alhrashi

Dr. Fadi's training did not stop at dental school. He completed an Advanced General Dentistry residency at the Eastman Institute for Oral Health in Rochester, New York, a residency that concentrates on the demanding side of general dentistry: oral surgery, implants, complex restorative work, and medically complex patients. Eastman recognized him with its Handelman Award of Excellence, given for clinical skill, leadership, ethics and communication.

He also completed a Dental Implant Fellowship at Tufts University School of Dental Medicine and is a Diplomate of the American Board of Oral Implantology/Implant Dentistry (ABOI/ID) and a Fellow of the American Academy of Implant Dentistry (AAID). Both are earned by examination and review of treated cases.

He remains a general dentist. The implant training sits inside a wider interest in comprehensive, restorative and surgical care: several failing teeth, a denture that never fit, a tooth that must come out and a plan for what replaces it.

How Dr. Fadi works

A visit with Dr. Fadi starts with listening: what brought you in, what has been tried, what worries you, and what you want your mouth to be able to do.

Then he examines every tooth, the gums, the bite and how old work is holding up. Any X-ray or 3D scan appears on the monitor beside you as he explains it.

The plan comes in plain language and in writing, with costs before anything starts. Complex cases are sequenced so you are never left without teeth you can use. If a simpler option will do, he says so. If a case belongs with a specialist, he refers you and explains why.

Calm is part of the method: each step explained before it happens, a signal to pause, breaks in longer appointments. Nitrous oxide is available, and other sedation options can be discussed after a review of your health and the planned treatment.

Seeing Dr. Fadi

Dr. Fadi is accepting new patients at our Bradlee Office Building location on W. Braddock Road in Alexandria, just off I-395 at the King Street exit, with free parking.

To book with him, book online and choose him as your dentist, or call 703-263-8719 and ask for Dr. Fadi. Our schedule runs 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM Monday through Friday and 8:00 AM to 1:00 PM on Saturday and Sunday. Say which days and hours suit you and the team will tell you when he is in. Second opinions on a plan quoted elsewhere are welcome.

Dr. Fadi speaks English and Arabic; the office also works in Spanish and Portuguese, so mention your preferred language when you book. Registration can be completed through the patient registration page before you arrive.

Straight answers

Does Dr. Fadi see children?

Yes. This is a family practice and both dentists see patients of every age. Book a child's checkup with either; if a child needs an extraction or a more involved repair, the team will match the visit to the dentist best placed to help. More on children's dentistry.

Which dentist should I book with?

Either, for checkups, cleanings, fillings and most everyday care. Where it helps to choose: Dr. Fadi for implants, extractions, dentures and complex restorative plans, Dr. Sameer Atrash for clear aligners, cosmetic work and digitally planned treatment. Records are shared, so seeing Dr. Sam for one thing and Dr. Fadi for another is normal.

Does Dr. Fadi place implants here?

Yes. Implant treatment is offered here, from the 3D planning scan through placement and the final tooth. Not every mouth is ready on day one: gum disease is treated first, there has to be enough bone, and he will tell you plainly if a graft is needed or a bridge or partial would serve you better. See are implants right for me.

Is Dr. Fadi a specialist?

No. Both dentists here are general dentists. Dr. Fadi's implant credentials are post-graduate qualifications, listed above exactly as awarded. When a case calls for a periodontist, endodontist, orthodontist or oral surgeon, we refer you and explain why.
