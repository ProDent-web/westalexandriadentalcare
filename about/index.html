About West Alexandria Dental Care

We are a new family dental office in the Bradlee Office Building, just off I-395, opening in October 2026. Two general dentists under one roof, open seven days a week from early morning. This page is the plain version of who we are, why the office is set up this way, and what happens when you come in.

Why we opened here

Dr. Sameer Atrash grew up in Fairfax, and his interest in dentistry began in high school through Fairfax County's Dental Careers Program. After a biology degree and a Master of Product Innovation at Virginia Commonwealth University, he earned his dental degree at Tufts in 2021. This office is the practice he wanted to build, close to home and set up around how families actually live.

The idea is in the tagline. Complete care, one dental home. Many families have one dentist for cleanings, another office for an implant, and a third for straightening teeth, each with its own forms and its own partial history. We would rather one team knew your mouth, and sent you to a specialist only when that is genuinely the better choice.

He opened the practice with Dr. Fadi Alhrashi, who brings nearly two decades of advanced training and clinical experience in restorative, surgical and implant care. Between them, nearly everything a family needs can be done in this building.

What no assembly-line dentistry means in practice

From the chair, it looks like this. The long version is on our approach.

  • Appointments are long enough to talk. Nothing is numbed until you understand what is being done and why.
  • You see what we see. X-rays and images come up on the screen beside you, and we point to the problem.
  • Costs come in writing before treatment starts, with your insurance estimate applied, so there are no surprise bills.
  • Watching is a real option. If a tooth can reasonably be monitored rather than treated, we say so.
  • No pressure and no selling up. When a simpler repair will do, that is what we recommend. Nervous patients get a comfort plan, not a lecture.

How a visit works

A first appointment usually takes 60 to 90 minutes. The full walkthrough is on your first visit.

  1. Booking

    You can book online 24/7, or call 703-263-8719 during our hours. Early, late and weekend slots are real options. Tell us your preferred language.

  2. Paperwork before you arrive

    Registration is online through our patient registration page, and you can upload a photo of your insurance card there.

  3. The conversation

    Before anyone looks in your mouth, we ask what brought you in and what you want from a dentist. Pain goes to the front of the line.

  4. The exam

    Every tooth, your gums measured, your bite, and an oral cancer screening. Digital X-rays are taken only when they will change what we do.

  5. The plan, in writing

    Healthy gums usually mean a cleaning the same day. Anything else comes as a written plan with costs and what can wait. You decide, at home if you prefer.

The office: Bradlee Office Building, off I-395

We are at 3543 W. Braddock Road, Suite E1, Alexandria, VA 22302, in the Bradlee Office Building, a few steps from Bradlee Shopping Center. From I-395, take the King Street exit. Free surface parking surrounds the building, so there is no circling and no meter to feed.

That puts us a short drive from Seminary Hill, Fairlington, Shirlington, Parkfairfax, Del Ray, Landmark and Van Dorn, Lincolnia, Bailey's Crossroads and South Arlington. Directions from each neighborhood are on our areas we serve pages, and a map is on the contact page.

Hours are 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM Monday through Friday and 8:00 AM to 1:00 PM on Saturday and Sunday. The technology inside is a short list used when it meaningfully improves care: 3D cone-beam imaging, magnification, digital X-rays and digital records. More on our technology.

Straight answers

Which insurance plans do you take?

Aetna, Ameritas, Delta Dental, Humana, Cigna, MetLife and Virginia Medicaid. Benefits vary by employer and plan, so we check yours before the visit. If your plan is not listed, call and we will check. See insurance we accept.

Do I need a specialist for an implant or a root canal?

Often not. Both of our dentists are general dentists, and much implant, root canal and surgical work is done here. When an orthodontist, periodontist, endodontist or oral surgeon is the better choice, we say so and refer you.

Can I choose which dentist I see?

Yes. Say so when you book and we schedule you with Dr. Sam or Dr. Fadi. If you have no preference, you get the first opening that fits. Adults, children and seniors are all seen here, so a family can keep its care in one place.
